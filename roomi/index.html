ROOMI

A home robot.

ROOMI is a small companion built for the home. It lives in your space, listens when you talk to it, and helps with the everyday things around the house.

The idea

  • Talk to it in plain language — no app, no menus.
  • It knows your home, so it keeps context between conversations.
  • Designed to be quiet and unobtrusive until you need it.
