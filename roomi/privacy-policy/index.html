Privacy Policy

How ROOMI handles your data.

Last updated: August 11, 2026

What we collect

  • Voice and command data — audio you address to ROOMI, and the text transcribed from it, so it can understand and respond.
  • Home context — the names, rooms, routines, and preferences you choose to set up.
  • Device and diagnostic data — model, software version, connectivity status, and error logs used to keep the robot working.

ROOMI does not record continuously. Audio is captured only around an interaction with the robot.

How we use it

Data is used to operate ROOMI — understanding requests, carrying out tasks, remembering your preferences — and to diagnose and fix problems. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Processing and storage

Processing happens on the device wherever it is practical. Some requests are sent to our servers when they need more compute than the robot has. Data in transit is encrypted, and stored data is kept only as long as it is needed to provide the service.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run ROOMI (for example hosting and speech processing), and only to the extent they need it. We may also disclose data if required by law.

Your choices

  • Mute the microphone at any time using the control on the robot.
  • Review and delete stored interactions and home context from the companion app.
  • Request a copy of your data, or its deletion, by contacting us.

Children

ROOMI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If a child has interacted with ROOMI and you want that data removed, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated in the app.
