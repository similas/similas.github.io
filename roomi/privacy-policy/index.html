ROOMI

Privacy Policy

Roomi is a personal voice assistant that runs entirely on a single device owned by its one user, who is also its developer. Data from connected services (such as WHOOP recovery and sleep summaries) is fetched read-only, used solely to answer that user's own spoken questions in the moment, and is not stored, logged, sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. There are no other users, no analytics, and no advertising.
